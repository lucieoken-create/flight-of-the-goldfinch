# Art direction

## Principle

Inside the painting the whole time, with real spatial bones. The target is approximately **80% painting and 20% reality**: believable perspective, anatomy, light, and depth beneath haze, grain, warm varnish, and restrained brush texture. A paused frame should read as a painting first and a photograph second.

## Palette

Night `#14100c` · panel shadow `#4c4032` · umber `#645c4f` · feather `#a0917a` · plaster mid `#c8bead` · plaster `#d8d0bf` · gold deep `#986111` · gilt `#c9a24b` · red wax `#70453c` · canal blue `#33445f`.

Canal blue belongs to Amsterdam. Gilt and red wax are rare narrative accents.

## Type

- EB Garamond: title and every quotation from Donna Tartt.
- Source Serif 4: opening credit, colophon, labels, and controls.
- No sans-serif.

## Image treatment

Generated environments carry painterly atmosphere while CSS supplies consistent grading, vignette, and bounded light. Avoid filters that flatten the gallery artworks or allow one scene's atmospheric layer to contaminate the next.

The bird is a European goldfinch with a red face, black-and-white head, warm buff body, and gold wing bar. The 33-frame source is more photographic than the environments; its small rendered scale, scene grading, and shadow should integrate it without erasing its anatomy.

## Real artwork rule

The five gallery works are composited from the supplied files and remain recognizable. Never generate or repaint them.
