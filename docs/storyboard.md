# Current storyboard

Six movements run inside one continuous scroll timeline. These names describe production structure only; they are not visible chapters or navigation.

## Opening — the painting

Begin in the dark with Carel Fabritius's painting. Establish the title and the single Donna Tartt credit. Move across the painted surface, encounter the chain, and let the bird leave the panel as the visitor scrolls.

## The Met

Morning museum light gives way to a darker emotional register without depicting a literal explosion. The bird carries the visitor onward.

## Hobart & Blackwell

Warm lamplight, clocks, furniture, frames, and visual density create the safest interior of the journey.

## Las Vegas

Bright, strange daytime color transitions into dusk. The Las Vegas quotation remains present through the colorful image. The bird crosses the environment without stopping the continuous flow.

## Amsterdam

The coldest and darkest environment: gabled houses, a complete lit bridge, black water, and modern houseboats. No literal snow or whiteout. Amsterdam fades away completely before the gallery.

## Gallery and return

The five real artworks appear in this order:

1. Ambrosius Bosschaert, *Vase of Flowers in a Window*
2. Frederic William Burton, *Hellelil and Hildebrand, the Meeting on the Turret Stairs*
3. Georgia O'Keeffe, *Cow's Skull with Calico Roses*
4. Claude Monet, *Le Grand Canal*
5. Georgia O'Keeffe, *Above the Clouds I*

The passage holds long enough to look at every work. One intentional empty frame follows. It becomes the portal back to the Fabritius painting, the closing quotation, and a colophon with “Return to the beginning.”
