# Current storyboard

Six movements run inside one continuous scroll timeline. These names describe production structure only; they are not visible chapters or navigation.

## Opening — the painting

Begin in the dark with Carel Fabritius's painting. Establish the title and the single Donna Tartt credit. Move across the painted surface, encounter the chain, and let the bird leave the panel as the visitor scrolls. Use the regenerated slender bird with the restored full-body pose animation as it leans, turns toward flight and opens its wings.

## The Met

Morning museum light gives way to a darker emotional register without depicting a literal explosion. The bird carries the visitor onward.

## Hobart & Blackwell

Warm lamplight, clocks, furniture, frames, and visual density create the safest interior of the journey.

## Las Vegas

Bright, strange daytime color transitions into dusk. The Las Vegas quotation remains present through the colorful image. The bird crosses the environment without stopping the continuous flow.

After the quotation, follow the bird up into the dusk sky. Carry the flight across the painted clouds, then descend toward Amsterdam. This approved September 29 passage connects the existing paintings and preserves both reading holds.

## Amsterdam

The coldest and darkest environment: gabled houses, a complete lit bridge, black water, and modern houseboats. No literal snow or whiteout. After the quotation clears, the bird leads the camera beneath the central illuminated bridge arch. Its stone passes around the view and reveals the first Bosschaert painting from shadow. The canal atmosphere clears before the gallery viewing holds.

## Gallery and return

The five real artworks appear in this order:

1. Ambrosius Bosschaert, *Vase of Flowers in a Window*
2. Frederic William Burton, *Hellelil and Hildebrand, the Meeting on the Turret Stairs*
3. Georgia O'Keeffe, *Cow's Skull with Calico Roses*
4. Claude Monet, *Le Grand Canal*
5. Georgia O'Keeffe, *Above the Clouds I*

Each artwork now has a longer stationary viewing interval, and all five fit within the gallery viewing height. The bird stays below them and the complete gallery quotation. One intentional empty frame follows. Its inner aperture expands around the visitor and reveals the empty perch. After passing the border, the camera retreats to the complete panel as the regenerated full-body poses fold their wings and land at the registered toe position. The final resting pose samples the original painting. The full Fabritius image returns after the landing, with an extended unobstructed hold before the closing quotation, then the colophon with “Return to the beginning.”
