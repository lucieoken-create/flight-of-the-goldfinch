# The goldfinch — storyboard v2.1 (decisions locked)

Six scenes, one continuous bird. Quote text is canonical in docs/quotes.md — this doc covers structure, bird action, and transitions.

**Locked:** 10 quotes used (Q5, Q11 cut). Q3 in the prologue. Q6 (Pippa) in. Q12 closes alone. Gallery finale: five works in journey order, Escher out. O'Keeffes and all artworks fully credited in the colophon.

## 00 · Prologue — the painting

Dark room. The Fabritius panel close up, brushwork visible. Slow drift across the surface on scroll. Q7, then Q3 as the drift finds the ankle and the chain gleams.
**Bird:** stirs on its painted perch; lift-off — the bird slips free, the chain slackening (first motif).
**Out:** the wall brightens into gallery light; dissolve to the Met.

## 01 · The Met, New York

Morning light, parquet, gallery walls. The lightest moment, deliberately. Q1 (his mother's line, spoken here, minutes before). Then the light changes: low rumble, dust drifting through light shafts, the gallery dimming — no literal explosion. Q2.
**Bird:** startled, erratic flight through the dust, then out.
**Out:** dust settles into warm lamplight; dissolve to the shop.

## 02 · Hobart & Blackwell — the antique shop

Lamplight, clock faces, chair backs, gilt frames. The safe place.
**Bird:** first real perch since the painting — atop a longcase clock. Q9 (Hobie). Q6 near a lit doorway deeper in the shop (Pippa).
**Out:** match dissolve on light sources — the lamp's glow blooms into desert sun.

## 03 · Las Vegas

Hard light, empty desert suburbs, pool blue, heat shimmer. The visual outlier, which is the point.
**Bird:** glides on thermals; perches on a chain-link fence (the chain motif, disguised in landscape). Q4.
**Out:** sun sets fast on scroll; neon rises; neon reflections smear into canal water.

## 04 · Amsterdam

Night, snow, black canal, gabled houses, lit bridge arches reflected (see Lucie's own blue-hour photos in scenes/amsterdam/). The darkest beat. Q8.
**Bird:** shrinks toward a single pale speck over the water — the faint spark made literal. Snow builds until the scene whites out. The whiteout is wordless; the snow does the talking.
**Out:** whiteout resolves into a bright gallery wall.

## 05 · Return — the gallery of loved things

Lucie's gallery: five works hung in journey order, each echoing a scene just lived:

1. Bosschaert, *Vase of Flowers in a Window* (Mauritshuis) — the Dutch golden age the Goldfinch was born into
2. Burton, *Hellelil and Hildebrand, the Meeting on the Turret Stairs* (NGI) — the unsaid "I love you"
3. O'Keeffe, *Cow's Skull with Calico Roses* — the Vegas desert
4. Monet, *Le Grand Canal* — the water cities
5. O'Keeffe, *Above the Clouds I* — flight itself

The bird flies the length of the gallery. Pinned long quote Q10; the scene holds while the reader reads. At the gallery's end, the one empty frame: continuous camera push (the single scrubbed-frame-sequence hero segment). The bird enters, lands on its perch; the chain settles gently. Q12 fades up as it becomes a painting again. Then colophon: the novel, Fabritius/Mauritshuis, all five artworks with museums, image sources, build credits.

## Motif tracking

The chain: prologue (slackens at lift-off) → Vegas (chain-link fence) → progress-thread UI (gilt line with scene stops) → the close (settles). The snow: named in the prologue (Q3), performed in Amsterdam (wordless whiteout).
