# Met and Las Vegas production images v1

Generated with the built-in image-generation workflow on 2026-09-09 and installed in the live experience.

## The Met

**File:** `public/assets/generations/met-v1.png`

A luminous, empty Old Masters gallery in early morning: stone-green plaster, herringbone parquet, restrained gilt frames, repeating doorways, long shafts of light, and suspended dust. The supplied Met images informed gallery character and architectural scale; the accepted shop calibration and Fabritius reproduction informed the painted surface and palette. Recognizable paintings were intentionally avoided.

## Las Vegas — matched pair

**Day:** `public/assets/generations/vegas-day-v1.png`

**Dusk:** `public/assets/generations/vegas-dusk-v1.png`

An elevated outer-Las-Vegas suburb with repeating stucco roofs, desert basin, mountains, distant Strip skyline, a single blue pool, and a chain-link foreground perch. The dusk image was edited directly from the day image with the camera and geography locked; only natural light, practical illumination, atmosphere, shadows, and reflections change. The distant city becomes colorful without turning the scene into a Strip postcard.

## Shared constraints

- Full-bleed 16:9.
- Approximately 80% aged oil painting and 20% believable photographic space.
- No people, bird, readable text, logos, or watermark.
- Quiet regions preserved for live quotations and a clear mid-frame flight path.
