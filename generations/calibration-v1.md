# Environment calibration v1

Generated with the built-in image-generation workflow on 2026-09-09 and installed in the live grey-box.

## Shared direction

- Full-bleed 16:9 environments for the scroll experience.
- Believable photographic space seen as an aged oil painting: approximately 80% painting, 20% photograph.
- Real perspective and light behavior beneath film grain, haze, warm varnish, lifted blacks, and restrained craquelure.
- No bird, people, text, signage, watermark, or legible faces.
- Preserve quieter regions for live quotation overlays and an unobstructed flight path.
