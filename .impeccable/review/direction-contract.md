# First continuous passage

Scope: local, code-led working study of painting → departure → Met → Hobart & Blackwell. The later scenes remain available as narrative scaffolding and are not claimed as finished continuous joins. No approved image comp; the existing environments and the user-approved treatment are the visual authority.

## User direction

Keep the concept; rethink the construction. Retain the environments unless a concrete replacement is discussed. Aim for cinematic movement through believable painted spaces. Use the persistent subject of the spider reference and continuous camera of the castle reference. Preserve every chosen quotation, the five real paintings, rewind and stillness, the complete mobile narrative, and the ambiguous return to home and tether.

## Design contract

- Full Fabritius panel and restrained editorial title in the opening.
- Warm plaster, umber, gold and near-black from the existing visual system; EB Garamond for literary text and Source Serif 4 for small supporting text.
- Readable, stationary quotation intervals. A phone puts the long prologue above the painting.
- One scroll playhead controls bird frames, path, camera, lighting and text.
- The Met doorway masks the shop; camera scale and position are calculated from its real aperture. The foreground passes around the viewer while the bird moves into the opening.
- Preserve original images. Use a separately generated empty-perch plate only as a compositing derivative during departure.
- A complete linear reading view for reduced motion, very short phone viewports, and image-loading failure. All five gallery images and quote text stay accessible.

## Quality bar and limits

The passage should demonstrate physical visual continuity between two retained environments. Typography must fit at 1280×720 and 390×844, quotes must remain exact, and reversing must produce the same composition. The existing flight cycle remains a study asset: its pale edges and limited heading range need a later bird development pass. Later destination-to-destination joins remain dissolves. Neither is presented as a final artwork.
