The goldfinch — art direction, v1

Style tile for the scrollytelling piece · palette sampled from the Fabritius panel · Aug 18, 2026

The principle

We are inside the painting the whole time — but the scenes keep their photographic bones. Roughly 80% painting, 20% real: real perspective, real light behavior, real detail, seen through a painted gauze — grain, haze, varnish — as if the world inside the panel had been filmed on old stock.

Type

"You see the mark, you see the paint for the paint, and also the living bird."

"…if a painting really works down in your heart and changes the way you see, and think, and feel — it's a secret whisper from an alleyway. Psst, you. Hey kid. Yes you."

— Donna Tartt · The Goldfinch   ·   02 · Hobart & Blackwell

EB Garamond (400/500, roman + italic) carries every word of Tartt's — big single-line quotes and the long pinned passages both. Old-style, bookish, near-Dutch. Source Serif 4 (400/600) is the apparatus: attributions, scene tags, colophon — always small, letterspaced, uppercase, like a museum placard. An all-serif system, no sans anywhere: the one modern anachronism the painted world doesn't need. (Karma, from the Fontjoy trio, was cut — its rustic texture fights EB, and this piece has no true body-copy role for a third face.) Scale: perfect fourth (1.333), base 18px.

The gauze — grade recipe

In the generations (the 80%)
muted oil-paint color, brushwork visible only in skies, edges and light · atmospheric haze in every scene · warm varnish cast (lifted blacks, no pure white) · craquelure allowed in flat areas · composition photographic: real lens perspective, believable depth
In the build (the 20%)
fine animated film grain overlay (2-3% opacity) · soft vignette · slight warm LUT via CSS filter on all scene layers · quotes stay live HTML — crisp type floating in the haze is itself the 20% real · Lenis smooth scroll + GSAP ScrollTrigger

Prompt boilerplate

House style — append to every scene generation

in the style of a 17th-century Dutch oil painting seen through fine film grain and soft atmospheric haze; muted palette of aged plaster #d8d0bf, umber #645c4f, old gold #986111, sealing-wax red #70453c; photographic perspective and realistic detail beneath a thin painterly gauze — 80% painting, 20% photograph; brushwork visible only in sky, edges and light; lifted blacks, warm varnish cast, subtle craquelure in flat areas; no text, no legible faces; cinematic composition, 16:9

the bird — sprite kit

european goldfinch (carduelis carduelis: sealing-wax red face, black-and-white head, gold wing bar), painted in the manner of Fabritius, on a plain transparent background; sequence of wing positions for a glide-flap cycle; states: lift-off, glide, landing, perched; consistent size and lighting across all frames + house style minus haze
