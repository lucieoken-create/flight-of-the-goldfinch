An experience inspired by and using quotes from
The Goldfinch by Donna Tartt

“There’s a doubleness. You see the mark, you see the paint for the paint, and also the living bird.”

“When I looked at the painting I felt the same convergence on a single point: a sun struck instant that existed now and forever. Only occasionally did I notice the chain on the finch’s ankle, or think what a cruel life for a little living creature—fluttering briefly, forced always to land in the same hopeless place.”

“I’d be perfectly happy if I could sit looking at the same half dozen paintings for the rest of my life. I can’t think of a better way to go insane.”

“Every new event—everything I did for the rest of my life—would only separate us more and more: days she was no longer a part of, an ever-growing distance between us. Every single day for the rest of my life, she would only be further away.”

“Still with real greatness, there’s a jolt at the end of the wire. It doesn’t matter how often you grab hold of the line, or how many people have grabbed hold of it before you. It’s the same line. Fallen from a higher life. It still carries some of the same shock.”

“She was the missing kingdom, the unbruised part of myself I’d lost with my mother. Everything about her was a snowstorm of fascination.”

“…I’d stopped myself from blurting the thing on the edge of my tongue, the thing I’d never said, even though it was something we both knew well enough without me saying it out loud to him in the street—which was, of course, I love you.”

“Ever since the painting had vanished from under me I’d felt drowned and extinguished by vastness… my painting swept away on that powerful current and drifting out there somewhere: a tiny fragment of spirit, faint spark bobbing on a dark sea.”

“…if a painting really works down in your heart and changes the way you see, and think, and feel… It’s a secret whisper from an alleyway. Psst, you. Hey kid. Yes you. An individual heart-shock… a really great painting is fluid enough to work its way into the mind and heart through all kinds of different angles, in ways that are unique and very particular. Yours, yours. I was painted for you.”

“That life—whatever else it is—is short. That fate is cruel but maybe not random… it is a glory and a privilege to love what Death doesn’t touch… And I add my own love to the history of people who have loved beautiful things, and looked out for them, and pulled them from the fire… singing out brilliantly from the wreck of time to the next generation of lovers, and the next.”

Flight of the Goldfinch

A personal response to The Goldfinch by Donna Tartt.

Selected quotations from the novel; all quoted literary text by Donna Tartt. Original paintings credited individually. Concept, personal reflections, and creative direction by Lucie Oken, with AI-assisted coding and select generated assets.

An independent project. No affiliation with or endorsement by the author, artists, museums, or rights holders is implied.
